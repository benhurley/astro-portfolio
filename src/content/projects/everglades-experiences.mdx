## Project overview

Everglades Experiences is a South Florida wildlife outfitter led by wildlife biologist and TV host Rosie K. Moore. They run guided night hunts for invasive Burmese pythons and a hands-on herpetology field program, sell fine art wildlife prints, and offer lodging and luxury hospitality for guests.

## Technical challenges

I customized a Liquid theme to match the client's design system and extended it with an anchored sub-navigation pattern that works on any page. Sections become anchor targets, so visitors can jump straight to pricing, schedule, or FAQ. We also took full advantage of the built-in CDN edge server that Shopify provides to serve a high-resolution video in the homepage hero banner, with no concerns over load time or SEO issues.

As their business grows, they can add new pages and configure new products for sale without needing a developer.
